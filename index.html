Why Campus Connect Exists

Navigating university life shouldn't feel fragmented. Between scattered WhatsApp groups, missed announcements, and outdated notice boards, students waste valuable time trying to stay in the loop.

Campus Connect brings everything into one unified digital ecosystem—making it effortless to trade securely, connect with course mates, and keep your finger on the pulse of what's happening around campus.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the platform.

Is Campus Connect completely free for students?

Yes, 100% free. It’s built entirely to keep campus life connected without any hidden charges or fees.

How do I access the web app?

Just click "Launch App" at the top or visit app.mycampusconnect.com.ng to jump straight into your feed, groups, and profile.

Can I buy and sell items safely?

Absolutely. The built-in marketplace lets you connect directly with trusted peers on campus for textbooks, gadgets, and dorm essentials.

Do I need to download an app from an app store?

No download required! Campus Connect is a fast web app that works straight from your browser, and you can easily add it to your phone's home screen.

What is the student survey for?

It helps shape what we build next! You can drop your feedback, ideas, or feature requests via survey.mycampusconnect.com.ng.

Who is behind Campus Connect?

It’s an independent platform built by students, for students, specifically designed to fix the fragmented ways we communicate on campus.
